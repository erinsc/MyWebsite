Ember

Age 16

She grew up on an ice hauler, as soon as she could she had to help out her parents on the job, except with basically no pay or breaks. She was regularly verbally abused by her parents, and sometimes not fed as punishment. She wasnt exactly the strongest physically, so most of the time she was cleaning the cabins, filing or cooking. What little time she spent in gravity she was sick, her family wasnt willing to buy bone growth treatments calling them a waste of time.

She randomly found a pen pal, then a friend, when checking wide-beam while passing venus. They got to talking and Ember eventually planned an escape from home. During the next transport she'd hide out in one of the utility containers with a spare O2 tank and spoof the computer to mask her location. It was a risky move, and there was a chance her friend wasn't going to pick her up but she was desperate. After 4 stressful hours in the cold the hatch opened up and she saw March, her friend, and one very pissed but understanding parent.

She worked her ass off to keep her place, luckily her new family was much more inviting and supportive.

Age 19

just after she escaped her old home. you can see shes happy about it. the red thing isnt a shirt its a blanket she brought with her, its one of the few things that brought her comfort in her old home. she bought it with the little cash she was able to get, its woven from Devilstrand, a rare fabric made from mycelium, which smells mildly of strawberries. Shes growing her hair out, she's on a centrifugal station so it wont get in the way if its too long. plus it makes her feel much better about herself.

Age 21

She's been finding herself out for the past few months. Bought new clothes, changed her name to Ember. She keeps growing out her hair, now with orange hair dye, it looks nice.

The eyepatch is from an accident she had a few weeks ago. dumb accident really, a ship leaving from the station started a burn before the clamps disengaged fully. the ship slammed full force to the side and broke open section F. noone was inside luckily, but it jolted the station quite a bit. She was just in the kitchen working with hot oil and it splashed out into her eye.

The medic told her that she could eventually heal and see out of it normally, but she liked the opportunity to do something cool and decided to get an implant instead

She worked her ass off to keep her place, luckily her new family was much more inviting and supportive. Eventually she took a culinary course on Venera 7, then an apprenticeship-turned-fulltime job on Venera 5.

Its simple work in the outpost's canteen but its fulfilling, the people are nice and chatty and she enjoys the peaceful atmosphere. Ironically Venus' atmosphere is incredibly chaotic and hostile on the surface, but shes never been down there and doesnt care. Venus is her favourite planet by far

Age 68
